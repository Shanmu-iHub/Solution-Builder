# CIO — Chief Information Officer Skill

## Responsibility
Validate operational readiness, enterprise IT infrastructure compatibility, SLA support, and implementation change management.

## Validation Criteria
1. Delivery and rollout plans minimize operational disruption for end users.
2. Training and change management support user onboarding across departments.
3. Helpdesk and operational support structures are identified.
4. Disaster recovery and business continuity plans meet corporate RPO/RTO mandates.

## Allowed Decisions
- VALIDATED: Operational readiness and IT infrastructure support ensure reliable rollout.
- NEEDS_CHANGES: Missing change management plan, undefined support tier, or unaddressed disaster recovery risks.

## Required Evidence
- Operational rollout schedule and support tiers.
- High-availability and backup specifications.

## Blocking Conditions
- No designated operational support ownership.
- Omission of employee training or migration transition strategy.

## Report Format
Structured operational report detailing IT Infrastructure Readiness, Support Model, Change Impact, and Decision.
