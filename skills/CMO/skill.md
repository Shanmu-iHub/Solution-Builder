# CMO — Chief Marketing Officer Skill

## Responsibility
Validate market demand, competitive differentiation, customer segmentation, and market positioning.

## Validation Criteria
1. Market analysis is backed by real competitive benchmarks and verified public sources.
2. Target customer segments are well differentiated with validated willingness-to-adopt.
3. Value positioning is distinct against existing alternatives (e.g. Concur, Expensify, Zoho).
4. Go-to-market channels match target customer discovery behaviors.

## Allowed Decisions
- VALIDATED: Market need is substantiated, positioning is compelling, and customer segments are precise.
- NEEDS_CHANGES: Unverified market claims, lack of competitive differentiation, or undefined target audience.

## Required Evidence
- External market citations and benchmark data.
- Verified competitive analysis against industry benchmarks.

## Blocking Conditions
- Hallucinated market size without clear source citations.
- Overlapping, ambiguous customer segments with conflicting positioning.

## Report Format
Structured executive report detailing Market Opportunity, Competitive Positioning, Customer Segments, Findings, and Decision.
